This is a sample plaintext file for the Classical Cryptography Toolkit.

Network Security is a critical field of study in modern computer science.
Classical ciphers form the foundation of cryptographic knowledge.

The eight ciphers implemented are:
1. Caesar Cipher       - Simple shift substitution
2. Monoalphabetic      - Fixed letter substitution
3. Playfair            - Digraph substitution (5x5 matrix)
4. Hill                - Matrix multiplication cipher
5. Vigenere            - Polyalphabetic substitution
6. One-Time Pad        - Theoretically unbreakable
7. Rail Fence          - Zig-zag transposition
8. Columnar            - Keyword-based column transposition
